Projects

Stanford University · 2026

FA-KGD

Frequency-aware Kalman-guided diffusion for MRI

A training-free diffusion sampler that makes MRI reconstruction frequency-aware through a Kalman-gated data-consistency step. Improves PSNR over ΠGDM across all fastMRI brain and knee settings, up to +0.51 dB at under 1% overhead.

Stanford University · 2026

FACT

Focused attention consistency training

A post-training method that makes language model behavior consistent by penalizing attention to adversarial cues. Reduced jailbreak success from 78% to 7% and sycophancy from 53% to 30% without degrading general capabilities.

Stanford University · 2025

Fairness in University Admissions

Integer programming under fairness constraints

Formulated admissions as an integer linear program with fairness constraints to analyze utility–equity trade-offs, showing that intersectional disparities persist even under single-attribute constraints.
