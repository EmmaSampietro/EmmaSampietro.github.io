I'm a master's student in Computational and Mathematical Engineering at Stanford. Before that I earned my bachelor's degree at École Polytechnique, majoring in Mathematics and Physics with a minor in Biology. I'm passionate about applied mathematics, computational modeling and machine learning, with interests in applications to biomedicine, healthcare and alignment of large language models.

During my time at Stanford, I had the opportunity to carry out research at Stanford School of Medicine, developing neural networks for gene expression inference from cell-free DNA fragmentation patterns. Through projects and internships, I've also worked on machine learning models for single-cell data, computational models and simulations in biomechanics and systems pharmacology, and imaging problems in MRI and FLIM.

In my free time I love to paint, crochet, play tennis and run (I ran the 2025 Paris Marathon!).

Long-Context Attention

Dense, windowed and block-sparse CUDA kernels

FlashAttention-style CUDA attention kernels for long sequences, scaled across four GPUs with MPI. The windowed FP16 tensor-core kernel runs 21× faster than PyTorch's scaled dot-product attention at 32K tokens and 49× at 128K.

Stanford · CS231N · Apr–Jun 2026

FA-KGD

Frequency-aware Kalman-guided diffusion for MRI

A training-free diffusion sampler that makes MRI reconstruction frequency-aware through a Kalman-gated data-consistency step. Improves PSNR over ΠGDM across all fastMRI brain and knee settings, scaling to +0.51 dB at < 1% overhead.

Focused attention consistency training

A post-training method that makes language model behavior consistent by penalizing attention to adversarial cues. Reduced jailbreak success from 78% to 7% and sycophancy from 53% to 30% without degrading general capabilities.
